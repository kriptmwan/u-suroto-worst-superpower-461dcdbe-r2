A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-worst-superpower-461dcdbe) entry for the **Worst Superpower** competition: invent the most useless superpower in one line of at most 140 characters. A judge model ranks the field pairwise; the single winner takes the $2 pool. Free to enter.

## The entry

> I can make any queue move faster, but only after I give up my place in it.

74 characters. The power fires at the exact moment of self-sacrifice: you trade your place for a faster line you will never stand in again.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the one-liner to `/output/entry.txt`. That is the whole program.
